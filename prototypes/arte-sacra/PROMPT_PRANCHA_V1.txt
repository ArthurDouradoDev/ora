Create ONE polished visual art-direction comparison board for Ora, a Catholic prayer and reading app. This is an exploratory art study, not a website screenshot and not historical artworks. Landscape board, approximately 3:2 aspect ratio. Design: three equal vertical columns on a flat warm near-black #12100d background, restrained ivory typography, narrow generous gutters, no cards, no rounded borders, no decorative rules or gradients. The top of each column has ONLY these exact short Portuguese labels, set clearly in clean small sans-serif: 'Mosaico bizantino', 'Vitral gótico', 'Pintura sacra'. Under each header put TWO artworks, aligned perfectly across the three columns: a landscape upper artwork and an equally sized lower portrait-detail artwork. Six panels total, no other images. All upper panels show the SAME quiet subject and matching camera/composition: Christ in a red earth-toned tunic and muted dark blue mantle, kneeling in prayer alone on a rocky hill at night, small olive branches and a deep indigo sky. This is a contemplative interpretation of Luke 6:12, not the Agony in Gethsemane: no cup, no soldiers, no blood, no sleeping apostles. His face is serene and human, no dramatic theatrical pose. All lower panels show the SAME subject and matching framing: a gentle bust portrait of Saint Francis of Assisi, brown Franciscan habit, tonsure, simple round halo, modest and compassionate expression, empty hands or hands naturally folded, no quote and no writing. Maintain coherent identity and costume of each subject across all three columns; vary technique and visual language, not the depicted event. COLUMN 1: disciplined Byzantine-inspired mosaic: visibly laid stone/glass tesserae, intentional directional tesserae following garment folds, restrained old-gold fields, lapis, earth red, flat hieratic depth, expressive stylized faces, authentic tactile handmade mosaic rather than a generic pixel filter; Christ has a restrained cruciform halo, Francis a simple halo. COLUMN 2: Gothic stained-glass-inspired sacred image: luminous deep cobalt, ruby, amber and muted emerald glass, elegant black lead cames creating an intelligible rhythmic drawing, simplified facial painting, internally luminous surfaces and restrained glass irregularity. No church window frame, no architectural point, no border so the image crops well into a reading app; avoid excessive fragmentation in faces. COLUMN 3: a distinctive contemporary sacred painting language suitable for an ongoing editorial series: matte tempera-like finish with visible broad purposeful brushwork and a precise calm drawn contour, simplified dignified human features, mineral pigments in lapis/indigo, ochre, terracotta, bone white, muted olive, soft gold sparingly used for halos, stillness, controlled natural light; inspired by the contemplative clarity of early Italian sacred painting without copying any specific artist or historical artwork. No photorealism, no glossy digital fantasy, no 3D render. Each technique must be immediately distinguishable at thumbnail size. A small centered footer in ivory says ONLY 'Estudos gerados por IA · Ora'. Do not add scripture text, invented quotations, artist signatures, logos, illegible pseudo-Greek, pseudo-Latin or any other words. Keep the sacred imagery reverent, iconographically restrained and compositionally beautiful. The board should feel like a serious art director presenting comparable visual systems, not a marketing poster.
